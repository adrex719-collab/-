# Target Data Model Principles

## Master data
Person, organization, branch, site, unit, asset and external party are single masters.

## Scope
Operational records carry organization/region/branch/site/unit scope as applicable.

## History
Historical records preserve source year/file/sheet/row and import batch.

## Identity
A person is not duplicated because of branch transfer or historical records.

## Audit
Material changes retain actor, timestamp and evidence.

## Classification
TEST/SEED, HISTORICAL and OPERATIONAL are distinct classifications.
